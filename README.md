# GENZVERSE V2 // Refactored Clean 3D Experience

Clean, high-impact editorial 3D web application for Gen Z Verse featuring the interactive 3D Meme Emoji mascot, Electric Lemon Yellow & Black/White aesthetic, and zero emojis.

## Live Development Server
Active right now at:
👉 **http://localhost:5174/**

## Key Updates & Features:
1. **Interactive 3D Meme Emoji Mascot (`meme_emoji.glb`):**
   - **Auto-Centered & Scaled:** Automatically normalized to sit prominently in the center of the screen without getting clipped or hidden behind UI.
   - **Full 360° Drag-to-Rotate:** Click and drag anywhere on the model to freely rotate it in 3D space with smooth momentum damping.
   - **Head & Eye Tracking:** Uses inverse bone rotation on `head_028`, `lEye_029`, and `rEye_030` to follow your mouse cursor smoothly.
   - **Single Click Animation:** Triggers animation (`EmotiGuy_ARMAction`) and audio feedback with spring squash-and-stretch.
   - **Rapid Click Combo / Hyper Spin:** Clicking 4+ times triggers **HYPER SPIN MODE** (360° backflip flips, 3x animation speed, audio effects, and combo indicator).
   - **Rotating Orbit Ring & Twinkling Stars:** Floating yellow and white starfield inspired by the rotating globe snippet.
2. **Palette Purge:**
   - Stripped away all pink, cyan, and neon clashing colors.
   - Strict color scheme: **Electric Lemon Yellow (`#ffe600`)**, **Black (`#050505`)**, and **White (`#ffffff`)**.
3. **De-Cluttered & No Text Overlap:**
   - Removed the extra 3D ferris wheel and rollercoaster clutter so the Meme Emoji has complete visual breathing room.
   - Clean, spacious layout with high-contrast brutalist cards.
4. **Direct Contact Info Removed:**
   - Completely removed all phone numbers and official email addresses.
   - Replaced with a clean, streamlined inquiry transmission portal.
5. **Zero Emoji Guarantee:**
   - Verified 100% free of Unicode emojis across all files.
